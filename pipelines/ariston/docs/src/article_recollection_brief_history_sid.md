# Recollection — "The Brief History of SID" — Ariston mention

The article mentions Ariston once:

> "Another commercial product in 1987, is Ariston editor. A scener who uses it
> is Demon (Neil Baldwin). He also used Electrosound."

This is the only reference to Ariston in the article. Key inference:

- Ariston is explicitly called a "commercial product" — it was sold commercially,
  not freely distributed.
- Neil Baldwin (handle: Demon) is documented as a scene user of the Ariston
  driver on the C64. Baldwin was primarily known for programming his own
  music driver, but apparently used Ariston for some works.
- "Also used Electrosound" — suggests Baldwin switched between drivers.

Note: HVSC does not classify any Neil Baldwin SIDs as Ariston-engine, which
may mean he used it for early unreleased work, or HVSC sidid signatures don't
catch his usage variant.
