# Ariston Composer Profiles — VGMPF Extracts

## Barry Leitch

Source: https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php/Barry_Leitch

Born: 27 April 1970, Paisley, Renfrewshire, Scotland.

C64 driver history (in order):
1. Electrosound 64 (initial)
2. **Ariston** — for Marauder (first C64 with both music + SFX)
3. Custom driver by unknown programmer (Turbo Ass), 1988–1989
4. Axel Brown's driver (Xenophobe)
5. Charles Deenen's driver (from 1991)

Quote: "Well on the c64 you were only as good as your driver."
Quote on Marauder: "the driver was very basic I really felt like I had
accomplished something in creating a piece of music that was complete."
First C64 game: ICUPS (music only); Marauder = first with music + SFX.

## Jonathan Dunn

Source: https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php/Jonathan_Dunn

Driver history:
1. **Ariston** — first two games (Subterranea + one other, delayed)
2. Martin Galway's driver — next four projects (at Ocean)
3. Paul Hughes' driver — later Ocean work, continuously refined
4. Own driver — final iteration used in Total Recall

The sound effect driver on his first two games was "likely Dunn's own" even
while music used Ariston.

Note: The VGMPF page explicitly says "It is unknown how Ariston was available
to Dunn and other users." This suggests the driver was not commercially
distributed but rather passed between composers informally.

## Matt Gray

Source: https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php/Matt_Gray

C64 driver history:
1. Soundmonitor (initial) + **Ariston driver** simultaneously — "sometimes
   optimized by game programmers and at that sometimes with declined quality"
2. Custom driver (Codemasters persuaded him to write his own)

Early C64 gameography (likely Ariston era, 1987):
- Driller
- Fruit Machine Simulator
- Hunter's Moon
- Mind-Roll
- Quedex

Later (own driver era, 1988+):
- Last Ninja 2: Back With a Vengeance
- Professional BMX Simulator
- 4 Soccer Simulators, Dominator, Maze Mania, Motocross, Treasure Island Dizzy,
  Tusker (1989)
- Deliverance: Stormlord II, Vendetta (1990)

## Allister Brimble

Source: https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php/Allister_Brimble

C64 driver history:
1. **Ariston** (= Wally Beben's driver) — early C64 titles
2. Michael Delaney's custom driver + editor — later C64 titles

Quote: "You'll probably notice that my earlier SIDS use [Wally Beben's driver]."
Successor editor features (Delaney's): "ADSR's, drum tables, vibrato's,
filters etc." — these features were presumably present in Ariston too, or
were improvements over it.

## Wally Beben

Source: https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php/Wally_Beben

Born: 5 September 1953. From Thetford, Norfolk (BBS Sounds Digital, August 1991).
Aliases: Hagar, Wal Beban, Wal Beben. Group: AddWare Ltd.

C64 work: ~25 games.
Amiga work: ~20+ games.
Atari ST: multiple games.

SID chip note: Used unstable filter on only one C64 game — Total Eclipse.

Amiga technique quote: "I wrote it (if I remember) initially using soundtracker
for which I had a little program I'd written to convert the data into blocks
that I could use within my own player that I'd migrated/converted from my C64
player." (On Amiga music composition post-C64.)

Atari ST rip format: labelled "WB" (Wally Beben) by retrospective rippers.
Original ST source code: lost (hard drive crashes).
