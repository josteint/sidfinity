# Ariston Composer Pages — VGMPF + HVSC Evidence

## Ariston Driver — Summary (VGMPF)
- **Creator:** Ian Crabtree, 1987
- **Co-developer:** Wally Beben (updates, helped write it)
- **Editor:** Philip Brabbin (GUI)
- **Assistant:** Charles Deenen (programmer — connection unclear, may be Maniacs of Noise link)
- **Language:** 6502 Assembly
- **Platforms:** C64 (original), Atari ST + Amiga (1988 ports by Beben, with help from "Chris from Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk")
- **Tuning:** Versions tuned at **424 Hz** or **434 Hz**
- **Composition method:** Most composers arranged directly in 6502 assembler rather than using the official editor
- **Maniacs of Noise connection:** In late 1987, MoN asked Beben about the "phasing" effect; Beben sent them the source code; they added better drums and sent it back. This produced the improved drum variant.
- **Composers (VGMPF):** Allister Brimble, Barry Leitch, Ian Crabtree, Jonathan Dunn, Matt Gray, Paul Meredith, Steve Barrett, Wally Beben

## Barry Leitch (VGMPF)
- **Ariston game:** Marauder (C64, 1988-06) — only Ariston game; it is unknown how he obtained the driver
- **Tuning:** "All tuned at 424 Hz (briefly 434 Hz in 1988)"
- **Method:** Typed notes and instruments into a 6502 assembler
- **Subsequent drivers:** Custom (Turbo Ass, then Axel Brown, then Charles Deenen)
- **HVSC Ariston SIDs:** 2 — Marauder.sid (init=$7FFA, play=$8002?), Captain_Courageous.sid

## Jonathan Dunn (VGMPF)
- **Ariston games:** First two games — Match Day II (C64, 1987-11) + Subterranea (C64, 1987, delayed)
- **Notes:** "It is unknown how Ariston was available to Dunn and other users"
- **SFX:** Likely Dunn's own sound effect driver alongside Ariston music driver
- **Subsequent:** Moved to Martin Galway's driver for next four games
- **HVSC Ariston SIDs:** 2 — Matchday_II.sid, Subterranea.sid
- **HVSC STIL:** Subterranea.sid (#2) titled "Jolly Rodger" by Jonathan Dunn

## Matt Gray (VGMPF)
- **Ariston use:** Initially used Soundmonitor and the Ariston driver
- **Quality note:** Quality sometimes "declined" when game programmers optimized his code
- **Transition:** Codemasters founders convinced him to write his own driver
- **HVSC Ariston SIDs:** 4 — Fruit_Machine_Simulator.sid, Mean_Streak_Loader.sid, Quedex.sid, Mean_Streak_v1.sid (Worktunes)
