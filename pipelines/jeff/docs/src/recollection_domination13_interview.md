## Key quotes

### On his music editor development history

> "I have been developing my own system during the years since 1991. I think I've done around 30 players and 2 editors."

He acknowledged the editors were never fully completed but was working on a new version he believed would "kick ass."

He previously used Future-Composer and Rock-Monitor but never adopted the JCH editor.

### On CyberZound Productions (CZP)

> "I started a music-label with Duck LaRock/CML in 1994 I guess, called 'CyberZound Productions' (CZP)."

Later members included: Mitch, Dane, Mindflow, Vip, Jadawin.

### Group memberships (as of ~1999)

Daniax → X-Factor → Camelot → CREST → Bonzai. Hiatus from scene 1997–1998.

### Technical detail

Jeff created approximately 30 different music players throughout his career — explaining why sidid detects multiple variants.
