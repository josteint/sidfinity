## Lemon64 Forum Thread

URL: https://www.lemon64.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=4872
Key finding from PVCF (user):
- Two variants of Reflextracker exist: "the 2-voiced digitracker" and "the LSD (synth duration editor)"
- QuadSID compositions "only can be recorded as a MIDI stream" — cannot be directly converted to .SID format
- A Polish Reflextracker competition disk exists
- Songs on "brainbeat4 side b" use Reflextracker
- A manual exists somewhere

**Technical note from PVCF:**
> "reflextracker (PC) song, with quadrasid, up to 10 channels to 3 channels [converted] with dmc and the changed polonus digieditor"

This confirms QuadSID songs (10 ch) need manual mixdown to 3 ch for standard C64 output.
