## Post 9 — pvcf (2003-06-30) — CRITICAL TECHNICAL QUOTE

> "its a reflextracker (PC) song, with quadrasid. it only can be recorded as a midi stream. however, it maybe would be possible to recompose them on a c64 with dmc and the changed polonus digieditor (used for centric -end digitune, for example). (after the crazy conversion of 'bladesweet' up to 10 channels to 3 channels its possible, i think)"

**Key technical facts extracted:**
1. **PC-based:** Reflextracker runs on PC, not on C64
2. **QuadSID support:** The new composition used QuadSID (multiple SID chips)
3. **No standard SID export for QuadSID:** QuadSID tunes "can only be recorded as a midi stream"
4. **Conversion path for 3-voice reduction:** Using DMC + the "changed Polonus digieditor"
5. **"Bladeswede" converted from 10 channels to 3 channels** — a real example of the conversion process
6. **"Centric end digitune"** was made using this conversion path
7. **Up to 10 channels** confirmed for QuadSID version

Also: PVCF mentions working on commercial music (PC/GBA/PS2/Xbox) after the C64 scene, and mentions hardware issues ("broken 1741 hardware" = likely a 1541 floppy drive).
