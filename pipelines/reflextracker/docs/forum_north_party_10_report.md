# North Party 10 (Warsaw, 2006) — Reflextracker Competition Report

Source: https://riversedge.pl/north-party-10-18-20-08-2006-in-warsaw

## Context

North Party was a Polish C64 demoparty. By 2006 (North Party 10), the Reflextracker was already
~11 years old but still used competitively by the Polish scene. The party featured a dedicated
**Reflextracker music competition** — confirming the tool had an ongoing dedicated user community
in Poland, not just a historical relic.

## Competition Participants

Three competitors are named:

1. **CJ Warlock** — his module "brought a lot of fun for attendances" (audience shaking their heads
   "like on metal concert"). The song was cut short by organisers due to "unnecessary screaming"
   from the audience's enthusiasm. CJ Warlock = Piotr Grabowski, the most prolific Reflextracker
   composer in HVSC (23 + 2 = 25 SIDs under handles "Warlock" and "CJ Warlock").

2. **Ramos** — his module "was playing for longest time from all 3 works." Not found in HVSC under
   this handle.

3. **V-12** (report author) — his entry "was cut in the middle. Many hours of work on the song
   ended up with the same [short play time] which slightly discouraged me from further
   participation." V-12 has 4 HVSC SIDs (No_Future, Never_More_Depression, Hardrave, Mix).

## Technical Implication

The fact that a demoparty in 2006 ran a dedicated Reflextracker competition category (rather than
a general SID/C64 music compo) means:
- The format was distinct enough from standard SID players to require its own competition slot
- The community had accumulated enough active users to fill a competition
- The PC-side authoring tool was still distributed and usable 11 years after its release

## Lemon64 Cross-Reference

PVCF's 2003 post in the Lemon64 "Reflextracker Stuff" thread specifically mentions "a disk with
a reflextracker competition from Poland" — this is the same tradition; Polish parties ran
Reflextracker competitions at least as early as ~1996-1997 (given the HVSC peak release year
of 1997) and at least as late as 2006 (North Party 10).

## Note on "BESCHREIBUNG" (Manual)

PVCF's 2003 post says "there should also be a manual for the tracker, if I remember right, but
dont ask where." The pouet.net comment by tomaes (2012) describes: "multi page instruction note
file written in German by PVCF." This manual was on the tracker distribution disk
(file name likely "BESCHREIBUNG" = German for "description/manual"). The manual was authored
by PVCF, not by kb or Quiss, despite them being the coders.
