Tunes in NewPlayer v15 - v20, 1990-1992.

This directory contains the rest of the music I packed and released as demo
tunes before stopping all C64 activities in 1992. I managed to make a few game
tracks for SilverRock and Domark, but all in all, the amount of game tracks
was disappointing. Very sad, since this meant a lot to me. In 1992, I put
myself into a minor musical crisis where I wondered if composing music really
was what I was meant to do. This made me stop all composing only to be kept
alive on my Korg M1, playing from 1992 to 1995. In 1995 I am trying to do some
tunes in FastTracker II on the PC, but I can feel that I should never have
stopped my musical activities...

CAVEMAN.DAT     Caveman

                Another cooperation between Morten Sigaard Kristensen and I.
                There are some funny leader instruments in this one - if you
                have the patience to listen to it...

CHMERANG.DAT    Chimerang

                A very long and samey tune, made for diskmagazines. After
                about 3 minutes of play the tune changes. Listen to the ring
                modulation leader from 04:39 - this is the best part.

CHORDIAN.DAT    Chordian

                A bombastic tune where I use ring modulation to the fullest.
                One of my favourites. This tune was sent to a guy in England
                who promised to use it in a game with credits by Vibrants.
                However, it seems as if he liked it soo much that he put his
                very own credits on the game...

COOPS_UP.DAT    Coops Ups

                Here Morten Sigaard Kristensen managed to make most of the tune
                and then "invited" me to do the solo in the end of it. However,
                the solo didn't really fit very well to the tune.

CRWDNOIS.DAT    Crowdnoise

                The last sound I ever did on the C64, made July 1992. At that
                time I had been away from the C64 for months, playing around
                with the PC - however, a guy in "Pretzel Logic" said he needed
                a crowdnoise effect, so I did it. The demo he worked on was
                AFAIK never released. This sound should be heard with full
                filtering, something that is not available in SidPlay at the
                time of writing this.

EUROWOOD.DAT    Eurowood

                Another sound effect. Drax made most of it but was not sure how
                he would emulate the bird noises, so I helped him out.

GOING_UP.DAT    Going Up

                A short introtune. I later made a better arrangement on the
                keyboard using the leader from 00:15. Maybe someday I will bong
                this into a PC tracker.

HAWAII.DAT      Hawaii

                A very long tune containing one of the longest solo parts I
                have ever done in a tune. The bassline and drums were originally
                made by Morten Sigaard Kristensen, but in the end I "raped" it
                so much that it virtually became my own work. The solo part
                starts at 01:39. Notice how the tune decays in the end - this
                was very unusual for a C64 song in general.

HEAVYHIT.DAT    Heavy Hit

                Short, "end-of-level-monster"-like tune made in a compromise
                player which took VERY little rastertime.

HOLLYWD.DAT     Hollywood

                A sad "she-is-dead" film-like tune. I still like this tune very
                much - it is samey, but it builds up just right.

HOOONING.DAT    Hooooning

                Conversion of a commercial on TV for OTA "guldkorn", a breakfast
                thing like cornflakes. It was to be used on the game of the
                same name, but the programmer ran out of memory space on the
                game cartridge. I believe the conversion is quite accurate.

HURRICAN.DAT    Hurricane

                Experimental tune testing the speed changing abilities of the
                new player v18. The change happens at 00:15.

KNAKDICK.DAT    Knackdick

                The only real cooperation tune released by Drax and I. I must
                admit however, that Drax made about 85% of the tune! ;)  The
                tune is quite varied and well worth listening to.

LADYLISA.DAT    Lady Lisa

                This tune was composed and then put on ice, only to be packed
                and released at a much later date.

MISJUDGE.DAT    Misjudge

                The second of the three tunes I did in the compromise player
                v19 which takes very little rastertime. Can you hear that this
                player in fact doesn't have a vibrato routine? :)

MNIAXION.DAT    Maniaxion

                Cute little introtune.

NINJACK.DAT     Ninjackie

                Either this one or "Maniaxion" (I can't remember which) was
                used on the titlescreen of a game by Domark.

POLICE.DAT      Police

                Another old tune put on ice only to be released much later. It
                was composed in a very fast speed setting.

REVENGE.DAT     Revenge

                Here I deliberately tried to put together a lot of chords in
                a song that shifts in and out of the minor and major scales.

SCANLINE.DAT    Scanliner

                First tune done in the compromise player v19. As mentioned
                before, this player has no vibrato routine at all.

STRIKE_F.DAT    Strike Force

                Conversion of the old Strike Force introtune - originally
                composed by "Diflex". I tried to be fair to the atmosphere
                of the original introtune.

TERMANAL.DAT    Termianal

                A short cooperation between Morten Sigaard Kristensen and I.
                As usual he did the bass and drums and I did the leader and
                chords.

TESTTUNE.DAT    Playertest

                As the name suggests a test of the miscellaneous functions in
                the new player v20, like speed- and puls programming test. This
                tune was of course never spread.

TRIANG1.DAT     Triangle1

                Attempt at making the SID chip say "Triangle" using ring
                modulation in three voices. Judge by yourself if you think
                I succeded! ;)  There are three attempts in all.
