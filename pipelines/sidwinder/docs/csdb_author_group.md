# TLC — Plus/4 World Member Profile

## Identity
- **Handle:** TLC
- **Real Name:** Levente Hársfalvi (inferred from Plus/4 World; not explicitly stated on profile)
- **Role:** Coder
- **Groups:** Coroners (CNS) — founding member; ex-member of Gods Of The Universe (GOTU)

## SIDwinder Role
TLC performed the Plus/4 conversion of Taki's original C64 SIDwinder V01.22, producing the V01.23 release.
Per Plus/4 World documentation: "Original Author: Balázs Takács (Taki/Natural Beat) - V01.22 for C64 (1994);
Plus/4 Conversion: Levente Hársfalvi (TLC/Coroners) - 2000"

TLC improved the editor and developed a new packer from scratch (the original C64 packer had the endpoint/glide bug).
TLC also released the fixed packer separately after Luca found the remaining bug in the V01.23 packer.

## Other Music Tool Releases by TLC
- SIDwinder V01.23 (2000-03-15) — Plus/4 port + enhancements
- TLC Digi V1.3 — digi music tool
- TLC Digi V2.0 — updated digi tool
- MConv V1.6 (2005-03-08) — music converter
- TLC Digi Future Composer Relocator (2002) — conversion tool
- Various other music converters

## Career Span
30 primary releases + credits on 117 additional projects, 1989–2025.

# Commodore Free Issue 91 — TEDzakker Article

Hermit (author of TEDzakker music editor for Plus/4) briefly mentions SIDwinder in context:

> "There's a port of SIDwinder (C64 by Taki, ported by TLC), a new PC editor Knaecketraecker,
> that's all."

This was Hermit explaining the landscape of Plus/4 music tools before TEDzakker's release — 
SIDwinder was one of the only native C64/Plus4 music editors available.
