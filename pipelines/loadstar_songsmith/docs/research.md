## Loadstar SongSmith (337 tunes in HVSC)

- **Author:** Joe Garrett (confirmed from b.songsmith loader, Loadstar 237)
- **Year:** c.2005 (copyright in loader); precursor by Garrett+Gardner c.1987-88
- **Documentation:** Moderate — Loadstar 237 has t.songsmith (79 lines), converter docs
- **Source available:** No public source. Binary on CSDb (Songsmith-Loadstar.d64, id=122855)
- **Notes:** Traditional-notation music editor (3 voices, W/H/Q/E/S duration keys,
  key+time+tempo). Files: m.SONGNAME (notes) + w.SONGNAME (ADSR+waveform, 1 block).
  Companion converters: SIDSmith (→SID Player .mus, Debby Cruz+Scott Resh, 1988),
  SmithSID (←.mus, Doreen Horne). Dave Marquis used SID EDITOR (Parsec), NOT SongSmith.
