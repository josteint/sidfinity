# comp.sys.cbm — "Text From The Brand New LOADSTAR #168" (1998)

This Usenet posting contains text from Loadstar issue #168, posted to comp.sys.cbm
in 1998. The post is from Fender Tucker (Loadstar managing editor, issues #47–199).

---

## SongSmith Precursor Reference

Q: Kenneth Barsky asked about songs from Loadstar issues #27 and #28.

Tucker replied (paraphrased):
> "Jim W. [Weiler] said that the songmaker they used in those days was written by
> Joe Garrett and Alan Gardner, and was the precursor to SongSmith."

Also noted:
> "[There are] probably programming wizards who could reverse-engineer the song codes...
> but there's little chance of anyone wanting to."

**Implications:**
- Issues #27–28 ≈ 1987–1988 (Loadstar launched 1985, monthly, so issue 27 ≈ late 1986,
  issue 28 ≈ early 1987)
- Joe Garrett and Alan Gardner wrote the PRECURSOR songmaker used in Loadstar #27–28
- SongSmith (proper) is described as the SUCCESSOR to this precursor
- The precursor song format is considered "reverse-engineerable" but obscure/uninteresting

## Dave Marquis — Uses SID EDITOR (NOT SongSmith)

Jack Barnes asked about ADSR parameters from "Trumpeter's Lullaby" in Anderson Jukebox II
(Loadstar #167). Tucker loaded the music file in **SID EDITOR** and reported:
> "Attack = 5 / Decay = 0 / Sustain = 15 / Release = 0"
> "[Parameters] change as the song goes on"

Tucker recommended users "study the actual editor to understand composer Dave Marquis's
technique."

**SID EDITOR** was available from: **Parsec, P.O. Box 111, Salem MA 01970-0111**

This is a commercial C64 music tool, separate from both SongSmith and the Compute's
SID Player. Parsec is not otherwise documented in community sources found in this session.

OPEN: Identify what "SID EDITOR by Parsec" is. Given the Salem MA address, this is a
US product from the late 1980s/early 1990s. This might be the same as or related to
the Compute's Enhanced Sidplayer "SID EDITOR" (which was sold separately from the player).

## Kenneth Barsky's Music Lists (LS #168 content)

Barsky created comprehensive song catalogs of all Loadstar music. Tucker condensed them
to ~300 blocks for the issue. Barsky offered full versions directly to readers on request.

## Anderson Jukebox (Loadstar #167 and earlier)

Referenced as "Anderson Jukebox II" — a jukebox player program by someone named Anderson
that plays Loadstar music files. This is a Loadstar-internal player.

---

## Dave Marquis — Role at Loadstar

Marquis is credited alongside Walt Harned and Fender Tucker for the FLAGS & ANTHEMS
series (national anthems collection). He is a Loadstar contributor, not a staff member.
His SID files are also distributed as MIDI, suggesting he composed on a MIDI/keyboard
interface rather than note-entry.
