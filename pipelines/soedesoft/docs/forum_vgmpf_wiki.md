# VGMPF Wiki — Jeroen Soede

Full URL: https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php?title=Jeroen_Soede

## Key statements

> "Jeroen Soede created a driver called Soundmaster to be able to arrange with drums like Rob Hubbard's. Michiel Soede created the editor."

Role split (matches Remix64 interview): Jeroen = driver/player code; Michiel = editor front-end.

> "For Amiga development, the brothers used 'their own driver and editor called SoundMaster Professional' along with a Kawai K4 synthesizer."

Amiga successor product name: "SoundMaster Professional" (distinct from "SoundMaster II" named in Remix64 — may be the same product at a later stage, or the professional/commercial release of SoundMaster II).

## RE-NOTES

1. "Arrange with drums like Rob Hubbard's" — this is the explicit design goal for the Soundmaster driver. Hubbard's drum technique (multi-layered arpeggio + waveform switching on voice 3 while voices 1–2 play melody) was the primary inspiration. Soundmaster was built to replicate this capability in an accessible editor environment.

2. The wiki does not provide game credits where Soundmaster was used for commercial game music — it focuses on demoscene use. No game company licensed Soundmaster for commercial products according to this source.
