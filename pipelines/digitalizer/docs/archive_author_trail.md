## Stein Pedersen — The "Borrowed Editor" Source

**CSDb URL:** https://csdb.dk/scener/?id=2272  
**Demozoo:** https://demozoo.org/sceners/1249/  
**Aliases:** Stone, The Idol, 1030  
**Country:** Norway  
**Groups:** Prosonix (1989–present), Panoramic Designs (1990–present), Offence (2009–present)  
**Rating:** 9.4/10 coder (10 votes), 9.4/10 musician (18 votes)

**Connection to Digitalizer:**
Olav Mørkrid admitted (Recollection #2) to having taken Stein Pedersen's music editor as a "freeze backup" and using it as the starting point for Digitalizer. Stein's editor is separately known as "SteinTronic" / "Prosonix Music Editor" (CSDb ID 179618). Stein Pedersen later joined Panoramic Designs (1990) alongside Olav — suggesting no lasting animosity.

In the V3.0 help text (June 1992), Olav acknowledges: "I would like to thank prosonix for inspiration (vi kaller det herming!)" — "herming" = Norwegian colloquial for mimicry/copying, used humorously.

Stein Pedersen also joined Funcom alongside Olav (mentioned in Recollection as a fellow scener who joined the company).

## Glenn Rune Gallefoss (6R6 / GRG) — V3.5 Co-Coder and Blues Muz Musician

**CSDb URL:** https://csdb.dk/scener/?id=8098  
**Demozoo:** https://demozoo.org/sceners/948/  
**Handles:** 6R6, GRG, Glenn, Glenn R. Gallefoss, Glenn Rune Gallefoss, Perts, Shark  
**Country:** Bergen, Vestland, Norway  
**SoundCloud:** https://soundcloud.com/glennrg-2

**Groups (current):** Nostalgia, SHAPE  
**Former groups:** Blues Muz' (1994–2011), Fairlight, Onslaught, Protovision, Calix, Collision, Digital Designs, Foxbat, Kraftverk, Pandora, Regina, The Freaks, Scene Plus Magazine Staff

**Roles:** Coder, Musician, Organizer, Public Relations Manager, Swapper

**C64 Tools credited:**
- **SID Duzz' It V1.3** (Apr 2001), **V1.5** (May 2002) — music editor maintained under handle "glennrg64" on SourceForge
- **Blues Muz' Player V1.0** (Jun 1994), **V1.1** (Aug 1994), **V6.4** (undated)
- **Digitalizer V3.5** (1995) — co-coder with Kjell Nordbo
- (DTZ2SDI converter CSDb credit is disputed — actual binary credits DJ GRUBY/TRIAD 2023)

**Connection to Digitalizer:**
- Credited in V2.2 (1989) and V2.5 (1989) disk images as `-GRG-` — present from the start
- Co-coded V3.5 (1995) alongside Kjell Nordbo, re-assembling V3.0 from source
- In V3.5, credited in the EDITOR binary with "2085 V3.5 BY GRG"
- Author of SID Duzz' It (SDI format) — the format targeted by DTZ2SDI converter

**SID Duzz' It history:**
SDI is a C64 music tracker created by Geir Tjelta + Glenn Rune Gallefoss of SHAPE, first released ~2001–2002 (V1.3), with major version 2.1.x from 2013–2014. Features: sequencer + tracker + sound editor; vibrato, pulse, filter, arpeggio, tempo. The name appeared first in Gallefoss's HVSC SIDs circa 1992 ("SID DUZZ IT"). The tool and format are distinct from Digitalizer; the DTZ2SDI converter bridges them.

## Geir Tjelta — Mozicart, SID Duzz' It Co-Author

**Demozoo:** (search for Geir Tjelta of SHAPE)  
**Aliases:** GT, Geir  
**Country:** Norway  
**Groups:** SHAPE, Mozicart, Offence

**Connection to Digitalizer:**
Acknowledged in V3.0 help text (1992): "Geir/Mozicart for helpful discussions." Geir Tjelta co-created SID Duzz' It with Glenn Rune Gallefoss as "Geir Tjelta of SHAPE." The Mozicart→SHAPE transition is documented in demoscene records. Mozicart and Prosonix were the two Norwegian music groups (besides Panoramic Designs) that Olav thanked explicitly.

**Technical innovation:** Invented a C64 SID realtime delay trick — recording SID channel 3 output and delaying playback as a "virtual 4th channel" echo effect. Published at chipflip.wordpress.com.
