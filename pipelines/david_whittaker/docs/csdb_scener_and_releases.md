## Selected HVSC SID Technical Data (addresses characterise player version)

| SID (game) | Year | Load | Init | Play | Size | Subtunes | Model |
|------------|------|------|------|------|------|----------|-------|
| Lazy Jones | 1984 | $1480 | $1500 | $1630 | 3328 ($D00) | 21 | 6581 |
| Treasure Island | 1984 | $C000 | $C6F5 | $C721 | 1868 ($74C) | 4 | 6581 |
| Panther | 1986 | $9000 | $9000 | $9151 | 3328 ($D00) | 1 | 6581 |
| Storm | 1986 | $9000 | $9000 | $911B | 2498 ($9C2) | 1 | 6581 |
| Red Max | 1986 | $E000 | $E000 | $E0A1 | 4096 ($1000) | 3 | 6581 |
| Max Headroom (preview v2) | 1987 | $8000 | $8000 | $80DA | 3056 ($BF0) | 3 | 6581 |
| Cosmonut | 1987 | $A400 | $A400 | $A538 | 2516 ($9D4) | 1 | 6581 |

**Observations:**
- All are 6581 PAL.
- Init address = load address in all observed cases (init does full setup, no separate boot entry).
- Play addresses are offset $111–$1D8 bytes into the module (player code header before data).
- Wide scatter of load addresses ($1480, $9000, $C000, $E000, $8000, $A400) — the player is relocatable or at minimum hand-placed per game.
- Lazy Jones (1984) is an early version: load $1480, 21 subtunes, 3328 bytes.
- Panther and Storm (1986) share load $9000 and nearly identical sizes — probably the same version.
- Red Max (1986) at $E000, 4096 bytes (larger than Panther) — possible later or alternate version.

## Bansai Cross-Platform Conversion Work

**Scener:** Bansai (CSDb ID 38332), functions: Coder + Cracker

**Releases:**
- "Xenon (ZX128 conversion)" (2023, Music) — converted Xenon's ZX Spectrum 128 music to C64
- "Lords of Sonics Music Editor" (2023, Tool) — Bansai provided code
- "Dragon Fighter: Into the Depth [2sid]" (2024)

**Technical work (from Lemon64 forum thread t=81385):**
- Performed an automatic conversion from Whittaker's ZX128 player to his C64 player
- Published converted Xenon music on DeepSID
- Found the players "quite compatible" — Spectrum version missing commands for SID-specific waveforms
- Confirmed arpeggio tables present in Spectrum player data (identical structure to C64)
- Quote: "I have the full player source/song data for Xenon if someone wants to attempt it"
- Working on running Whittaker's NES player on C64 backed by a 2A03 → 2SID translation layer
- Quote: "His player does all the heavy lifting for effects, not the PSG in the 2A03, so it seems reasonable that it can be emulated without any conversion necessary"
- Key insight: **Whittaker's NES driver was derived from his C64 driver** — the player semantics are almost entirely in software, not hardware.
