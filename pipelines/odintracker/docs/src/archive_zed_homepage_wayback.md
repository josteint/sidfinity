**Personal homepage:** http://www.inf.bme.hu/~zed/ ("Enter the site of Zoltán Konyha")
**Email:** zed@inf.bme.hu

The site was active from at least 2000-05-17 (earliest Wayback snapshot) through 2018-01
(last snapshot). The tracker page had these sub-pages:

- `intro.html` — Overview and screenshot
- `news.html` — Release timeline
- `faq.html` — FAQ (10 questions)

- `contact.html` — Contact info

## Intro Page Content (from 2001-04-14 snapshot)
> "This page is the official home of Odin Tracker, a new music editor for C64.
> Just bring back the good old days of happy C64-tracking!
> The program features MOD-like song structure, no DUR commands.
> The pattern editor should be quite easy and comfortable to use, that is the
> main goal of the tracker."
>
> "The player however is nothing advanced and altogether is painfully slow.
> If you wish to use some song in a demo you may need a faster playroutine.
> Any ideas for speedups are welcome."

### Utils: Dat2Sid
Converts packed OdinTracker songs (+ player) into .SID files for SIDPLAY.
Works with .DAT, .PRG, .D64, .P00 files. Supports multispeed.
- dat2sid-1.2-win32.zip (12kb) — Windows (requires MFC)
- dat2sid-1.2-linux.tgz (35kb) — Linux (requires Qt 2.x)
