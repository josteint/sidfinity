# OdinTracker — General Community Forum Mentions (Aggregated)

This file collects scattered community mentions that did not warrant
individual files.

## 1. Woolyss.com chiptracker listing

Source: https://woolyss.com/chipmusic-chiptrackers.php?s=Odin+Tracker
Content date: archived ~2007

Classifies OdinTracker as:
- Platform: Commodore 64
- Sound chip: MOS Technology 6581
- MIDI support: No
- License: Free
- Description: "A popular chiptracker for Commodore 64 (C64)."

## 2. comp.sys.cbm "Electrosound 64" thread (2002-09-17)

Source: https://groups.google.com/g/comp.sys.cbm/search?q=odin
Thread by Rob Adlers / Mike Paull, 2002-09-17

Snippet (from Google Groups preview):
> "tracker-based tools like Odin or Cybertracker are probably just as easy to use"

Context: Respondent comparing Electrosound 64 (a non-tracker music editor)
to contemporary tracker tools. OdinTracker and CyberTracker cited as the
two representative "tracker-based tools" for C64 as of 2002.

## 3. comp.sys.cbm "making / Tracking music on c64" thread (2001-01-20)

Source: https://groups.google.com/g/comp.sys.cbm/search?q=odin
Thread by Fitnak / Hukka, 2001-01-20

OdinTracker mentioned in context of native C64 music trackers. Hukka
(Joel Toivonen) is a known OdinTracker composer (~6 SIDs in HVSC). Full
thread text not recovered.

## 4. Lemon64 "Midi Trackers?" thread (t=26647)

Source: https://www.lemon64.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=26647

OdinTracker appears in a list of ~80 C64 tracker programs. No discussion
of its features. The thread asks whether any C64 tracker can send MIDI
output — no MIDI support for OdinTracker was confirmed (matches source:
no MIDI code in vplayer.s).

The SID Preservation tracker documentation page does not include

## 8. Chordian.net C64 editors comparison table (2018)

Source: http://chordian.net/c64editors.htm
Content date: February 2018

OdinTracker is NOT included in the 13-editor comparison table. Likely
absent because it was discontinued in 2001 and no longer under development
at the time of the comparison. The comparison focuses on actively maintained
trackers.

## 9. SounDemoN CSDb profile

Source: https://csdb.dk/scener/?id=1257

SounDemoN (Otto Järvinen, Finland) is listed as providing music for
OdinTracker 1.11, 1.12, and 1.13 releases. He is confirmed as affiliated
with Church 64, Dekadence, and Onslaught. His notable achievement per
CSDb: "made a 4 rasterline player."

## Overall community assessment

- OdinTracker was recognized as one of two major native C64 trackers
  (alongside CyberTracker) in the 2000–2002 period.
- It was described as "user-friendly" compared to alternatives.
- Key differentiator cited: "slidearps" (combined slide+arpeggio instruments).
- Key limitations cited: drum sound quality, no samples, no dual SID, no MIDI.
- No technical format writeup exists anywhere in the community — all format
  knowledge comes from the source code itself (vplayer.s, defines.s, help/).
