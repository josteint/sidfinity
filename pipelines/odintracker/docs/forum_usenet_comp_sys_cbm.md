# Usenet: comp.sys.cbm — OdinTracker Mentions (2000–2002)

Three threads in Google Groups' comp.sys.cbm archive mention OdinTracker.
Full thread text was not recoverable (Google Groups renders partially); only
search-result snippets and partial thread content are captured here.

## Thread 1: "SID Tracker?" — 2000-09-06

Authors: Witchy (OP), Lars Forsberg (respondent)

Snippet (from search result preview):
> "Take a look at **Odin Tracker**. You can find"

Context: The OP asks for a SID tracker for creating and editing SID music on
the Commodore 64, analogous to Soundtracker on the Amiga. Lars Forsberg
recommends OdinTracker.

This places OdinTracker as a known community recommendation as early as
September 2000 — 7 months after the v1.00 release (February 2000).

## Thread 2: "making / Tracking music on c64" — 2001-01-20

Authors: Fitnak (OP), Hukka (respondent)

Hukka is a C64 musician known to use OdinTracker (HVSC has 6 Hukka SIDs
classified as OdinTracker). This thread is about creating music on real C64
hardware. OdinTracker mention appears in context of usable C64-native trackers.
Full text not recovered.

## Thread 3: "Electrosound 64 - anyone with a D64 image?" — 2002-09-17

Authors: Rob Adlers (OP), Mike Paull (respondent)

Snippet:
> "tracker-based tools like **Odin** or Cybertracker are probably just as easy
> to use"

Context: Discussion of the Electrosound 64 music editor (a different, older
C64 music tool). Respondent compares it to contemporary tracker-based tools,
citing OdinTracker and CyberTracker as the two obvious alternatives.

This classifies OdinTracker alongside CyberTracker as the established C64
native tracker options as of 2002.

## Summary of Usenet evidence

- OdinTracker was recommended by community members as a SID tracker as early
  as September 2000.
- It was known as a "tracker-based tool" (vs. dedicated music editor/sequencer)
  alongside CyberTracker as of 2002.
- No announcement posts from Zed himself found in comp.sys.cbm archives.
- No technical format discussions found on Usenet.
