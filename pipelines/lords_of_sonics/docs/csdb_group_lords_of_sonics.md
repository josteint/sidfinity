## Notes

- LOS was a two-person group (Schneider + Blidon) that existed ~1988–1989
- Markus Schneider moved on to X-Ample Architectures in March 1989
- The group's output was primarily music releases, using CP Verlag / Golden Disk 64 / Game On publishing platforms
- The music editor they created (later called "Parsec Music Editor") was shared informally and became the foundation for Compotech
- No aliases or related group links are listed on the CSDb group page

## Relationship to CP Verlag / Golden Disk 64

Several LOS tunes were published via CP Verlag's Golden Disk 64 and Game On magazine disks, which were commercially distributed in Germany. This explains the professional distribution of amateur scene music.
