## Other web findings

### VGMPF Wiki — Markus Schneider
- **URL:** https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php?title=Markus_Schneider
- Full career overview: born 1970, Winsen, Lower Saxony. Got C64 in 1984, entered demo scene by 1987.
- Met Jens Blidon during a school project week at **Hermann-Billung-Gymnasium**.
- "Spent 2 months in 1988 writing him a better sound driver" after Blidon was dissatisfied with SoundMonitor. This driver became the foundation of the Parsec Music Editor.
- Collaboration with classmates led to first game commissions.
- X-Ample Architectures invited Schneider in 1989; he spent "7 weeks on it" merging his sound driver with X-Ample's version, producing Compotech.
- Games: Rolling Ronny (Virgin), Lethal Zone, No Mercy, Xiphoids, Timezone (Kingsoft).
- Amiga work used Chris Huelsbeck's TFMX (given to Schneider for free).
- Earnings: 500–1500 DM per composition.
- Later: co-founded Skycom GmbH (1998), started C64 remixing (2002), co-founded Symphonic Dreams (2005) with Rob Hubbard.

## Technical notes extracted

### Binary identification signatures (from sidid.cfg)
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cadaver/sidid/master/sidid.cfg

**LordsOfSonics/MS main signature (Compotech branch):**
```
79 ?? ?? 0A A8 B9 ?? ?? 9D ?? ?? B9 ?? ?? 9D ?? ?? AC ?? ?? BD ?? ?? 99 ?? D4 END
AND
AC ?? ?? AD ?? ?? 29 04 C9 04 F0 ?? BD ?? ?? 99 01 D4 BD ?? ?? 99 00 D4 BD ?? ?? 3D ?? ?? 99 04 D4 END
```

**`(Parsec)` sub-signature:**
```
9D ?? ?? 9D ?? ?? 9D ?? ?? CA 10 E5 A9 ?? 8D ?? ?? A9 01 8D ?? ?? A2 18 A9 00 9D 00 D4 CA 10 FA 60 A9 ?? 8D 18 D4 A2 02 8E ?? ?? CE ?? ?? 10 06 END
```

**`(Compotech_V2.x)` sub-signature:**
```
A9 ?? 8D ?? ?? CE ?? ?? 10 ?? A9 ?? 8D ?? ?? A2 ?? 8A 4E ?? ?? 90 ?? 20 ?? ?? ?? ?? 69 07 AA ?? 15 90 ?? A9 ?? 09 ?? 8D END
```

**Observations from signatures:**
- The Parsec signature shows an init routine that clears SID registers (`A2 18 A9 00 9D 00 D4 CA 10 FA` = X=24 loop writing 0 to $D400+X down to $D400) and then sets master vol (`8D 18 D4` = STA $D418). This is a clean universal reset pattern.
- The Compotech V2.x signature starts with `A9 ?? 8D ?? ??` (LDA#, STA) then `CE ?? ?? 10 ??` (DEC, BPL = tempo/counter decrement), followed by `A2 ??` (LDX) and `8A 4E ?? ??` (TXA, LSR = shift-register for noise/pulse?), then `90 ?? 20 ?? ??` (BCC, JSR = conditional subroutine call). The `69 07 AA` sequence (`ADC #$07, TAX`) and `09 ?? 8D` (`ORA, STA`) suggest waveform flag assembly.

### Player family structure
- The HVSC sidid engine name is `LordsOfSonics/MS`.
- The family covers at minimum two distinct format generations: **Parsec** (1989) and **Compotech** (1990/1992/1995).
- **Compotech V2.1** (1995) is a third version, credited to Chap Bizarre + Joachim Fräder + Markus Schneider.
- **XTracker V4.1x** (1996, by Tufan Uysal / SoNiC) is grouped under the same sidid family name, but is from a different author. It may share format compatibility or use a Compotech-derived player routine.

### Development history
1. 1988: Markus Schneider writes first sound driver for Jens Blidon (Lords of Sonics era). Duration: ~2 months. Reaction against Soundmonitor limitations.
2. 1989: Driver evolves into **The Parsec Music Editor V5.1** (released via Mnemonic Designs). Co-coders: Nic and ADT. SMC fixes bugs and writes documentation.
3. 1989: X-Ample Architectures approaches Schneider. He joins March 1989. Spends 7 weeks merging his driver with X-Ample's version.
4. 1990: **Compotech** released by X-Ample Architectures. Credited to Markus Schneider & Helge Kozielek.
5. 1992: Compotech re-released / new version (the CSDb entry at id=122614).
6. 1995: **Compotech V2.1** released by X-Ample Architectures (Chap Bizarre + Joachim Fräder + Markus Schneider).
7. 1996: **The Ultimate X-Tracker V4.1x** by Tufan Uysal (SoNiC) — apparently format-compatible enough to share the sidid family classification.
