# "The Companion to the Commodore 64" — Keith Bowden, Pan Books, April 1984

## Bibliographic facts
- Title: *The Companion to the Commodore 64*
- Author: Keith Bowden
- Publisher: Pan Books (a Pan Macmillan imprint)
- Published: **April 1984**
- Pages: 208, paperback
- ISBN: 9780330284790
- Topic: covers programming, graphics, and **sound** on the C64.

## Why it matters

This is the **single root source** of the entire Companion family.
Bowden's book ships a working 3-voice SID music driver as a *type-in
listing without an editor* — readers manually entered the player code +
song data into their machines and could play music by running the listing.

The historical record is unambiguous on three points:

1. **Hubbard's pre-Monty C64 work** (Up Up & Away, Commodore 64 Music
   Examples tunes 2-15) is a direct extension of the Bowden listing.
2. **Chris Murray's player** (Henry's House, English Software 1984) is
   an independent extension of the Bowden listing — different freq table
   (A4 = 423 Hz).
3. **Clever Music's player** (used in CRL releases 1985-87) is yet
   another independent extension of the Bowden listing, later rewritten
   by Steven Chapman, Jay Derrett, and probably John McPhee.

## What to find next

The author Keith Bowden has an academic page at Birkbeck
(http://www7.bbk.ac.uk/tpru/KeithBowden.html) — same Keith Bowden? The
book exists in the Centre for Computing History collection. **Getting a
scan of the book's music chapter would be the highest-value next step**:
it would give us the *unextended* Bowden driver as ground truth, against
which Hubbard's, Murray's, and Clever Music's extensions can be diffed.

Search the Internet Archive / archive.org for the book — it has not been
located in this session but type-in books of this era are routinely
archived there.
