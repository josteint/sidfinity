## Third-party developments on Brian's work

### Fenek (Arise, Protovision) — GMC V2 Unfinished (CSDb #44814, 2006)
Fenek disassembled the GMC V1.0 player binary and reimplemented both editor and player
from the disassembly, removing the 16-instrument limit and producing a "more streamlined,
DMC-resembling editor." This confirms the GMC V1.0 player is machine-code only (no source
ever released), but is disassemblable and reimplementable. Fenek's reimplementation is the
closest thing to a documented format spec available from a third party.

### Wacek (Arise) — GMC 0.5x (CSDb #193964, 2020)
Adapted the GMC player for 25 Hz (half-PAL) playback; released at 25Hz Music Compo 2020.

### The Ancient Temple (TAT) — GMC V1.6 100% (CSDb #156855, 1992)
Bug-fixed V1.6 by Case of TAT; most complete preserved binary for V1.6.

## Source availability

**None.** Brian has never publicly released the GMC or DMC source code. The only
near-source artifacts are:
- Fenek's GMC V2 reimplementation (from disassembly — not Brian's original code)
- The CSDb downloads of the tool binaries (D64 disk images)
- The HVSC SID files (player binary embedded in each SID)
