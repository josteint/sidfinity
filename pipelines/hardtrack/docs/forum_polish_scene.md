# Polish C64 scene forums — HardTrack Composer (provenance + culture)

These threads on **c64scene.pl** confirm HardTrack's standing and distribution
in the Polish scene. They contain little hard format detail but pin down
provenance that frames the version question, and they name active HardTrack
composers (useful for cross-checking HVSC author handles).

Polish manual (Nov 2010).

> EN: "I bought Hardtrack in the cassette version at some shop on Piotrkowska

> [Commodore & Amiga magazine]."

**Provenance facts:**
- HardTrack was **sold commercially on cassette in Poland** (not only a scene
  freeware release), distributed by **TIM-SOFT** (per the Allegro archive
  listings "POLSKI EDYTOR MUZYCZNY HARDTRACK COMPOSER TIM-SOFT").
- It was the favourite editor of multiple Polish musicians — confirms the large
  HVSC footprint is Polish-scene-driven.
- Active HardTrack users named: **wackee (Arise)**, **Klax** (also #197 tunes in
  the top-user list in research.md). Cross-reference for HVSC author attribution.

## c64scene.pl t=123 — recommendation context

> **V-12 — 2009-03-26 21:06**
> PL: "może na dobry początek bym polecał klasycznego Hardtrack Composera"
> EN: "perhaps as a good start I would recommend the classic HardTrack
> Composer"

Context: V-12 recommends HardTrack as the **beginner-friendly** C64 editor
versus the more complex Sid Duzz It (SDI) and Cybertracker. Confirms HardTrack's
reputation as an approachable, widely-taught editor — consistent with its broad
HVSC presence and many casual composers.

multispeed and instrument details are NOT discussed on the Polish boards in
these threads — those live in (a) the c64power.com V2.0 dev thread
(`forum_c64power_v2.md`) and (b) the Polish user manual
("Hardtrack Composer — podręcznik użytkownika", archived on chomikuj.pl).
