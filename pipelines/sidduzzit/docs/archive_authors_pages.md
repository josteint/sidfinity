## Primary Authors

### Geir Tjelta (handle: GT)

- **CSDb scener ID:** 1266
- **Country:** Norway
- **Active scene period:** 1989 – 2010 (CSDb last entry)
- **Earlier handle:** Predator (January 1988 – November 1989)

**SDI contributions:** Original coder of all V1.x releases (V1 1996, V0.98, V0.98A).

music editor with different format, NOT related to SDI's binary format; the two share
authorship but not data structures. GT mentions using a jingle from that editor in
"Daze Before Christmas" (SNES, 1994).

## Usage Rights

From the `SDI.2.1.6-docs.txt` (official documentation, dated 2013-05-18):

> "You are allowed to use the music player in a game free of charge, but you will
> have to credit us for writing the music player" — either the software name or
> author credits acceptable.

This means SDI player code is **free to use** but **requires attribution** to
Geir Tjelta and/or Glenn Rune Gallefoss / SHAPE.
