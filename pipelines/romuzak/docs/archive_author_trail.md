## Digital Marketing — The Company

Oliver Blasnik operated or was the primary developer at a German software company called
**Digital Marketing**. This is NOT the modern concept of internet marketing — it is a small German
C64 game developer/publisher active 1988–1991.

### Stefan Hartwig's Connection to Digital Marketing
Stefan Hartwig (C64 musician, 54 tunes in HVSC using RoMuzak, the largest user count after Ass It)
described in his Remix64 interview: "...before my work for Digital Marketing, Digital Excess and
Starbyte Software began." This places Digital Marketing as one of three publisher relationships for
his C64 game music career.
Source: Remix64 interview with Stefan Hartwig (https://remix64.com/interviews/interview-stefan-hartwig.html).

### Thomas Detert (X-Ample) Connection
Thomas Detert composed "Theme for Digital Marketing" and "Digital Marketing Intro" (both 1989) for
Commodore 64 — these are standalone SID music pieces for Digital Marketing's promotional materials.
The X-Ample Architectures demo group (Detert + Helge Kozielek + Michael Detert) developed
Hydrogenese and Bamboo for Digital Marketing under contract.
Source: Demozoo scener 46171 (Thomas Detert); CSDb search for "Digital Marketing".

## Duotris — Musician Credit Ambiguity

The GoodPSID database labels the Duotris in-game SID as "Oliver Blasnik (Medien Verlag)" but the
Lemon64 entry credits musician as **Jörg Schäfers** (published by Game On / CP Verlag). These are
conflicting — GoodPSID credits Oliver Blasnik for composing the tune; Lemon64's credits may reflect
the main game credits. The publisher "Medien Verlag" vs "CP Verlag" discrepancy is also unresolved.
OPEN: Verify which musician actually composed the Duotris SID by checking the SID header in HVSC.
