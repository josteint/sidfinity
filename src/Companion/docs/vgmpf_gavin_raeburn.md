# Gavin Raeburn (Gaxx) — separate engine, but co-credited with Derrett

## Biography

- Born April 26, 1968, UK (Scotland).
- Influences: Ultravox, Visage.
- ~1986: learned machine code on ZX Spectrum, then moved to C64.
- **June 1987 to 2009: Codemasters.**
- 2010: founding member of Playground Games (still there).

## Music driver

- **Custom C64 driver tuned at A4 = 433.5 Hz** — note: this is
  DISTINCT from any of the three Companion variants (Bowden/Hubbard
  440Hz, Murray 423Hz, Derrett unknown).
- 1988: enhanced version using PDS (Programmers Development System),
  sampling drums and speech at 12015-13137 Hz, 4-bit.
- His SID outputs sound near-identical across SID chips (8580 vs
  6581) except for samples.
- NES: wrote his own sound driver, used DPCM channel with waveforms
  from a Casio CZ-101. Allister Brimble later wrote a tracker that
  exports to Raeburn's NES sound engine.

## Why he matters here

Gun Runner (1987, The Power House) is co-credited Raeburn & Derrett
and sidid classifies its SID as `Companion/Jay_Derrett`. So the
final delivered SID was assembled with Derrett's engine, not
Raeburn's. The Raeburn driver is a SEPARATE engine that should
not be confused with Companion — they merely share a credit on one
release.

## Gameography (relevant)

1987-1993 across multiple platforms: Rock Star Ate My Hamster,
Bee 52, Micro Machines, the Quattro series (C64, NES, CPC, ZXS).
