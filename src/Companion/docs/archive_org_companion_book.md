## Content (what we could determine)

The Computing History catalogue entry does not include a full table of
contents, but cross-references confirm:

1. The book covers C64 programming including graphics **and sound**
   programming (per the search-result summary text).
2. The seed tune "Roundabout" by Keith Bowden, (c) 1984 Pan Books, is
   archived in HVSC and uses a player that sidid identifies as
   `Companion`. This strongly implies the book contains either the
   full driver source or a closely related listing.
3. JC64dis explicitly recognises "Keith Bowden's player" as one of
   its example engines, grouped with Hubbard's "Companion player"
   and Chris Murray's player.
