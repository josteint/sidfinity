# Up Up and Away (Starcade, 1984) — GTW64

## What it says about the music

> Gwyll Jones: "Rob Hubbard (I think that was the name of the person we
> sub-contracted to) only coded the Music – he had no copyright to the
> music code either as he was paid up front."

> Tom Jones: "I designed the games and Ron coded the music."

> Tom Jones (on length): "At the time all music-bites had to be
> restricted to less than 10-15 secs duration to avoid copyright claims
> from the composers."

## Driver/engine info
- No explicit "Companion" or "Bowden" mention here. GTW64 only
  identifies the *commercial* relationship.
- Chris Abbott (linked in the comments thread) references that the
  Synth Sample III driver is "a further evolution of the driver used in
  Up, Up and Away" — i.e. Hubbard's *post*-Up-Up-and-Away driver was
  already a step beyond the Companion-derived driver he used here, and
  this would be the lineage that eventually became the Monty-on-the-Run
  family driver.

## Timing
- Game itself: 1984 Starcade title, ported from Atari to C64 in ~2 months
  (1983-1984).
- This makes "Up Up and Away" almost certainly **Hubbard's first
  commercial C64 release**, and the SID file with that name in HVSC is
  tagged `Companion` by sidid — therefore the **canonical primary
  binary** for the Hubbard-flavoured Companion variant.
