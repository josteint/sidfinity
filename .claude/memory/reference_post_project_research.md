`docs/post_project_research_ideas.md` (written 2026-08-22, from a
conversation during the DMC f2 closeout). NOT work and NOT scheduled — a
parking lot, explicitly subordinate to the grind.

What it holds, so a future session knows whether it is worth opening:

- **Lineage is TWO maps.** Player-CODE phylogeny comes from the
  extraction tooling (`engine_fingerprint.py`, `dmc_canon_diff.py`), NOT
  from USF — USF abstracts the player away by design. Musical lineage
  (covers, remixes, an author's style across editors) is the USF half.
- **The wedge census is already scene ethnography**: carrier counts
  separate a circulated group player-build (hundreds of carriers) from a
  one-off hack (1-3), and the in-place same-length byte patches identify
  the *method* (freezer-cartridge monitor, not source reassembly).
- **A preservation finding worth publishing**: ledger C29's class means
  a nonzero number of these pieces are NOT self-contained works — they
  sonify banked-in KERNAL ROM, psiddrv's patched vectors, the power-on
  RAM stripe. Composition and machine state are not separable, and we
  have a measured list.
- **Honest limits** to state before any claim: coverage is DMC-shaped
  today (the largest families have no pipeline), HVSC credit metadata is
  inherited uncertainty, hacks have no provenance.
- **Borrowed vocabulary** (stemmatics, collation, urtext, homoplasy,
  vestigial, founder effect, holotype, taphonomy, chaîne opératoire,
  emic/etic, the comparative method, Ship of Theseus) — each mapped to
  the thing it names in this project. Useful for framing a write-up or
  talking to historians/musicologists.
