# Hubbard SID catalog

All SIDs in HVSC where Rob Hubbard is credited as composer or co-composer,
sorted chronologically. **97 total** across 9 release years.

Engine classification comes from `sidid` plus a probe with our `rh_decompile`
(succeeds on the "classic" early-engine family — Commando, Monty, etc.).

## Counts by engine

| Engine | Count | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| **Rob_Hubbard (early/classic)**  | 83 | The engine our `pipelines/commando` and `pipelines/monty` codegens target. |
| Jason_Page/RobTracker            | 6 | Later tracker that emulates Hubbard's style. |
| SidTracker64                     | 5 | Modern (post-2017) iOS tracker reissues / reconstructions. |
| Companion                        | 2 | Early Hubbard tool, pre-dates his own player. |
| Rob_Hubbard (variant)            | 1 | sidid identifies it as Hubbard but `rh_decompile` fails. Only one: the Lieblich co-comp. |

## Notes

- The 1984 entry (*Up, up & Away!*) and the 1985 *Commodore 64 Music Examples*
  use the **Companion** engine — these pre-date or coincide with Hubbard developing
  his own player. They are not in scope for our current pipelines.

- *Chop n' Drop* (1988, with Russell Lieblich) is the lone "variant" — sidid
  identifies the Hubbard player but our `rh_decompile` cannot parse it. Likely
  Lieblich's code structure is the dispatch and Hubbard contributed only the music
  data. Filed under `MUSICIANS/L/Lieblich_Russell/` in HVSC.

- The post-2017 entries (*Casio Extended*, *Don't Step on My Wire*, etc.) are
  modern reconstructions made in SidTracker64 (an iOS app) or Jason Page's
  RobTracker — they reuse Hubbard's name in the author field but the player code is
  the modern tracker, not Hubbard's original 6502.

- The 83 "classic" entries all use the same early-engine family. Two are
  already rebuilt by our pipelines; the remaining **81 candidates** could go through
  a cloned pipeline tomorrow with minimal new code, modulo the per-song quirks we
  haven't hit yet (other fx_flags bits, table-arp variants, multi-speed timing).

- Generated by `docs/build_hubbard_catalog.py` (combines PSID header parsing,
  `tools/sidid`, and `pipelines/commando/extract/decompile.py`). Re-run when the
  HVSC mirror is updated.
